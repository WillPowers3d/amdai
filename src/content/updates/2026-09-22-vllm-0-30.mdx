Dated digest (2026-09-22, America/Chicago). ROCm **10.0.0** is still the R9700 (`gfx1201`) inference pin — [release notes](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/docs-10.0.0/about/release-notes.html) and the live [compatibility matrix](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/install-on-linux/en/latest/reference/system-requirements.html). AMD’s 10.0 ecosystem still lists **vLLM 0.27**.

Community cutting-edge Docker tag moved: `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:v0.30.0` (Docker Hub, pushed ~2026-09-22Z). Keep **`v0.29.0`** as the previous try-tag (`wheels.vllm.ai/rocm/0.29.0/rocm723` still documents that week). Do not mix 0.27 and 0.30 in one venv. Default PyPI `vllm` is still CUDA.

Named Qwen defaults from this week were **later withdrawn**. Qwen3.5 / 3.6 **27B** and **35B-A3B** stay multi-GPU territory — a public llama.cpp multi-GPU writeup is not a single-card recipe.

Honesty unchanged: `TORCH_BLAS_PREFER_HIPBLASLT=0` on training resets; SGLang AITER may fail; prefer llama.cpp HIP/Vulkan over Ollama on gfx1201.
