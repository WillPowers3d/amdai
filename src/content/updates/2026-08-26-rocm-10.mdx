AMD’s 10.0.0 notes (26 Aug 2026) put **Radeon AI PRO R9700** on the device table as **gfx1201**. Framework table in that release: **vLLM 0.27**, **SGLang 0.5.15**, **PyTorch 2.13**. Use the versioned [release notes](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/docs-10.0.0/about/release-notes.html) and [transition guide](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/docs-10.0.0/about/transition-guide-TheRock.html) if you are reconstructing this week.
