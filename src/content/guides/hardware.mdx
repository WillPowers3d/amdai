## PSU

Cited board power for more than the R9700, plus dual-card honesty: [Power and PSU](/power). Buying a used 6000- or 7000-series card: [Buying a used Radeon](/guides/buying-used-radeon).

750 W is AMD’s *minimum recommendation* for the card in a generic system, not “headroom for a 5090-class CPU and six drives.” Use a quality unit with a **native** 12V-2x6 / 12VHPWR cable if that is what the card expects. Cheap pigtails are how people learn about transient spikes.

## Cooling and noise

Many AI PRO cards are compact **blower** designs that exhaust out the bracket. Good for a cramped workstation; loud at the desk when memory clocks sit at the top P-state. Leave the adjacent slot empty. Do not bury the intake in cables. Watch junction and memory temps with `rocm-smi` **while generating**, not at the GNOME wallpaper.

Open-air test benches lie about case airflow. A small cube case will teach you about recirculation.

## Case and risers

A short dual-slot PRO card fits places a triple-slot 7900 XTX does not. A PCIe 4.0 x16 riser will usually *work*. Decode-bound chat may not care; fat prefill might. Prefer a direct x16 slot if you are chasing prefill.

Firmware: [BIOS settings](/bios) — Above 4G Decoding, Resizable BAR / SAM, CSM off, the x8/x8 versus x16/x4 split, EXPO/XMP, IOMMU. Chipset facts are cited. Per-board BIOS rows stay empty until someone submits one. Boards whose maker manuals document a CPU-lane x8/x8 split: [dual x8/x8 motherboards](/motherboards).

## VRAM versus model size (approximate)

Weights + KV cache + batch share 32 GB. **Verify per file and context.** These are planning brackets, not promises:

| Situation | 32 GB often means |
| --- | --- |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q5_K_XL, 262K, f16 KV | Our one-card daily driver — see [stacks](/stacks) and the [R9700 measured page](/gpus/r9700) |
| Same at Q8 / even longer cache | Tight; measure. Q3 is estimate/community; Q1/Q2 stay off. |
| Qwen 3.8 Flash UD-Q6_K_XL | Publisher file ~169 GB — expect RAM to hold most of it on 32 GB |
| Dense 70B | Aggressive quant, short context, or another GPU |
| MoE with few active experts | File size can surprise you — read the card and the [calculator](/calculator) |

A 16 GB RX 9070 XT is the better buy if you live in smaller Qwen. The R9700 is the 32 GB bracket. Q3 is listed as estimate/community — do not drop to Q1/Q2 to “make it fit.”

Host RAM: our test rig is **256 GB DDR5**. 64 GB is a reasonable home minimum next to one 32 GB GPU. Flash at UD-Q6_K_XL is a ~169 GB publisher file — that box needs a lot of RAM. 32 GB host gets ugly fast.

## What not to optimize first

Do not buy a second card for “double tok/s” before you have [one GPU proven](/tutorials/verify-rocm). Multi-GPU at home pays an RCCL/topology tax. ROCm wants matching CPU-connected widths (x8/x8 or x16/x16), not a mixed leftover. See the [dual / multi-GPU guide](/multi-gpu), [BIOS](/bios), and the [weekly honesty list](/).
